50sen

A teh o that costs less than the parking.

A 50 sen teh o was the signboard promise that opened the doors in Taman Nusantara. It became the reason a whole housing area learned the words "boat noodle". A decade on, prices for everything else have moved. This one is still printed on the 2025 menu.

Hot or cold, 50 sen either way, in a proper cup.
Taking it away? Big cup, RM2.00 hot or RM2.50 cold.
No minimum order. Walk in, drink one, walk out. Some do.
Signature plate

The blue is real.

Bunga telang, steeped the same morning, colours the rice. Around it: ulam from the market, kerisik, a spoon of budu, salted egg and keropok ikan. It is a Kelantan plate with a Southern Thai accent, and it is the one visitors from Singapore photograph before eating. From RM9.90.

Good to know

Questions we get on WhatsApp.

Is it halal?

Yes. Both kedai are Muslim-owned. No pork, no lard, no alcohol.

Do you take reservations?

Yes, on WhatsApp, for groups of eight or more from 3pm onwards. Larkin: 018-328 4149. Gelang Patah: 018-760 4149. Send the date, time and headcount.

Which kedai is nearer from Singapore?

By the Second Link (Tuas), Gelang Patah is about ten minutes from the CIQ. By the Causeway (Woodlands), Thamada Village in Larkin is about fifteen minutes from JB Sentral.

Do you deliver?

Both kedai are on GrabFood. Thamada Village Larkin is also on foodpanda. Pickup orders on WhatsApp at both.

When are you closed?

Every Tuesday, both kedai. Gelang Patah closes at 9pm, Larkin at 10pm. Before a public holiday, check Instagram for that day's hours.

Is service charge included?

Menu prices are before 10% service charge and 6% SST. E-wallet and cash accepted at both kedai.
