50sen

A teh o ais that costs less than the parking.

A 50 sen teh o was the signboard promise that opened the doors in Taman Nusantara. It became the reason a whole housing area learned the words "boat noodle". A decade on, prices for everything else have moved. This one has not.

Brewed strong, served in a proper glass, not a plastic cup.
No minimum order. Walk in, drink one, walk out. Some do.
Born at Gelang Patah, still poured there every day the kedai is open.
Signature plate

The blue is real.

Bunga telang, steeped the same morning, colours the rice. Around it: ulam from the market, kerisik, a spoon of budu, salted egg and keropok ikan. It is a Kelantan plate with a Southern Thai accent, and it is the one visitors from Singapore photograph before eating.

Good to know

Questions we get on WhatsApp.

Is it halal?

Yes. Both kedai are Muslim-owned. No pork, no lard, no alcohol.

Do you take reservations?

Yes, on WhatsApp. Send the date, time and headcount to 018-328 4149 (Larkin) or 018-760 4149 (Gelang Patah).

Which kedai is nearer from Singapore?

By the Second Link (Tuas), Gelang Patah is about ten minutes from the CIQ. By the Causeway (Woodlands), Thamada Village in Larkin is about fifteen minutes from JB Sentral.

Do you deliver?

Thamada Village Larkin is on foodpanda and GrabFood. Both kedai take pickup orders on WhatsApp.

When are you closed?

Every Tuesday, both kedai. Gelang Patah closes at 9pm, Larkin at 10pm. Before a public holiday, check Instagram for that day's hours.

Cashless?

Yes. E-wallet and cash at both kedai.
